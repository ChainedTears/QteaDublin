Our Story

A family shop,
tea first.

Qtea started with a simple idea: a drink is only as good as the tea underneath it. We brew from loose leaves in the shop. Our house oolong is a dark roast from Taiwan, with a quiet savory depth and a sweet, lingering finish, and it's the backbone of our Roasted Oolong Milk Tea.

Every drink comes in six sweetness levels, so you can have it exactly as you like. Something to eat? Our kitchen makes basil popcorn chicken, curly fries and Spam musubi.
